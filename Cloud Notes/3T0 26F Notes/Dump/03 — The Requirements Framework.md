# Lecture Notes
## Stakeholder Expectations
Stakeholders (individuals impacting or is impacted by a project) have a direct stake within a project with their own *Needs*, *Goals*, and *Objectives*. These are informed by **Requirements** and **Evaluation Criteria**.

> Needs, Goals and Objectives (NGOs) are part of Stakeholder Expectations, 
> > These can also be negotiated, and updated throughout the process. 
> 
> 
> **Requirements** and **Evaluation Criteria** would be then part of the design requirements, informed by the NGOs in the process. 
>
