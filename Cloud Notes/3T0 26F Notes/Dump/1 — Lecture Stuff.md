## Principles of Engineering: 
Based on the ideas of $3T9$ in the Toike Oike; confirmed in 1989. 
1. $F=ma$
	1. Or generally; $\Sigma F = ma$; The idea is that we want this to be 0 (if it isn't; things are moving). 
2. You *cannot* push on a rope. 
	1. Every equation really is just an approximate model. 
	2. Equations aren't fully sufficient; and cannot model everything. They can simplify situations well enough (and if you account it as well), but they cannot fundamentally account for everything. Principles of Engineering: 
		1. More succinctly; this is due to buckling (i.e. under stress; materials plastically deform) 
3. In order to solve any sufficiently advanced engineering problem, it's a sufficient condition to know the answer. 
		1. Or in short-form: To find the answer, you must know the answer. 
	1. This is defined by experience; to determine the conditions within a engineering project, you must have some kind of experience in it already.
