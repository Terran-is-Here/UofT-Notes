# Lecture Notes
## Arguments 
Recall from[[1 — The Praxis Design Framework, PRA01 Debrief#Black-Box Model / "Dam" Model| the Dam Model]] that engineers partake in arguments whenever they try to design things: 
* The nature of reality itself is often disputed. 
* Our models make an argument to what we end up perceiving. 
* Choices in designs are also an argument.
* Getting building permissions to construct designs also include an argument. 
* The *acceptance* of work within reality also requires an argument.
Arguments are omnipresent in all of the segments of Engineering Design. Making decisions and recommendations require arguments: 
* Decisions require justification, 
* Recommendations require proof.
