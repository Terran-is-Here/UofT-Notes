> Question 2: 
> A particle of mass $2.0\ kg$ moves under influence of the force $F(x) = \frac{3}{\sqrt{ x }}$. If it's speed at $x=2.0\ m$  is $6.0m/s$, what is it's speed at $x=7$? 
> 

$$
\begin{align}
W = \Delta K &  = \int_{6}^7F(x) dx \\
 & =\left[ -\frac{3}{2(\sqrt{ x })^3} \right]
\end{align}

$$
