# Structural Engineering
The __art__ & **science** of **designing** and making structures with economy and elegance; so that they can **safely** **resist** the forces which they **may be** subjected to. 

> [!caution] Note
> **These notes will be mainly for **



> [!note]- Definitions
> **Art:** 
> The intuition one has to design a cool thing; or rather an innovative thing. 
> 
> **Science:** 
> The technical knowledge behind the design of structures; i.e. the backing theories as to what forces they can withstand. 
> **Design:** 
> How things should be actually shaped? 
> **Making:** 
> The act of physically constructing structures; structures that cannot be constructed aren't good. 
> **Economy:** 
> A structure should be affordable; knowing the cost of structures is vital to get them to actually be built. 
> **Elegance:** 
> Structures can look good and look stylish; as well as being functional.
> **Safety:** 
> Something should be safe to help maintain the responsibility of the general population. 
> **Resist:** 
> Structures should be able to safely *resist* the forces subjected to them; i.e. being able to withstand the forces subjected to them. 
> This is mainly gravity, but could include wind; thermal; etc. 
> **May be:** 
> Sometimes we can't fully plan out everything; we need to balance out what topics to prioritize and focus on.
