## What *is* research? 
* The general point of research is to uncover new information. 

In terms of how it could look like: 
* Theoretical / Computational
	* In this case, this would be like sitting and modelling a model and seeing if it correlates with pre-established models and or theoretical models. 
* Experimental (Lab) 
* Observational (Field / Clinical) 
	* This would be going into a field, getting information / data to which apply a model to in order to infer information. 
* Technique / Instrument Development

Recall that Research is not purely isolation, Research *and* Development exists as an Acronym for a reason (where these focus more on industry-applicable research) 

### Undergraduate Research
Great way to learn and apply theory / learn new theories! 
* Field-specific skills (instrument operation / process / techniques in field) 
* General technical skills (dealing with large datasets / using technical software) 
	* e.g. matlab, etc. 
* Critical Thinking!
	* Uncertainties (and how to handle them) 
	* Decision making + Considering multiple factors
	* In other words, trying to approach an unknown problem with multiple possible angles independently! 
	* Distinguishing things basically.
* Professional Skills!
	* Communications, Prooject Management, Teamwork and Collaboration!

### Finding Funding
* EngSci's generally have ESROP (Engineering Science Research opportunities Program) 
* Three Streams: 
	* Global (partner institutions around the world (mid December deadline)
	* U of T (student-sought opportunities at UofT) (early March deadline) 
	* ExOp: (student-sought opportunities outside of UofT) (early March deadline) 
* More info will be at EngSci Info Hub on Quercus.

* NSERC  (Undergraduate Student Research Award) 
	* ~6,000CAD + 1,125 CAD
	* Canadian Citizen / PR applicable
	* 16 Consecutive weeks of research! 
	* Deadline: Early March 2027 (TBA) 
	* (normally Year 2-4 priority)

* UTEA (University of Toronto Excellence Award) 
* Center for International Excellence (CIE)
